# Partiva Design System

## Direction

- Personality: precise, human, practical and trustworthy.
- Product world: automotive parts, workshop flow, inventory labels, graphite metal and petroleum-blue enamel.
- Density: dense enough for daily operations, with generous breathing room on public pages.
- Signature: a geometric petroleum-blue P symbol uses a negative-space mechanical profile beside a forward-leaning Space Grotesk wordmark; the separate mascot is a confident teal-and-graphite mechanical mouse mechanic.

## Palette

- Petroleum teal: #0F7079 for primary actions, active states and brand presence.
- Deep petroleum: #0A5961 for hover and high-contrast brand surfaces.
- Graphite: #172B32 for headings and primary text.
- Paper white: #FFFFFF for cards, forms and clean work surfaces.
- Light steel: #F3F6F6 and #EAF0F0 for the canvas and grouped surfaces.
- Muted graphite: #64777C for supporting copy.
- Amber: #D89A4B for attention and operational highlights.
- Green: #3B9A78 for completed or healthy states.

Use color to communicate state or action. Keep the page mostly neutral and reserve teal for the next meaningful action.

## Typography

- Wordmark: Space Grotesk 700 italic, lowercase partiva, with tight optical tracking and a subtle forward skew; paired with the standalone `assets/partiva-symbol.svg` mark.
- Product headings: Manrope 700–800, tight tracking.
- Body and interface labels: DM Sans 400–700.
- Type hierarchy: 11px metadata, 12–14px interface copy, 17px supporting copy, 30–50px section headings, 44px+ public hero.

## Depth and surfaces

- Use surface shifts and quiet borders as the primary depth strategy.
- Keep borders low-contrast: #DCE7E7 on light surfaces.
- Use soft shadows only for elevated cards, modals and floating UI.
- Sidebar and canvas should feel like one workspace; separate them with a quiet border.

## Spacing

- Base unit: 4px.
- Common scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64.
- Dashboard density: 8–16px inside controls and cards, 24–38px between work areas.
- Landing-page rhythm: 64–88px between major sections.

## Component patterns

- Primary button: petroleum teal, 44–50px high, 10–11px radius, clear verb.
- Secondary button: white surface, quiet border, graphite text.
- Operational card: white surface, 1px border, 9–18px radius depending on context.
- Mascot: use only as a supporting guide or welcome element; never merge it with the wordmark or symbol. The source asset is `assets/partiva-mascot.png`.
- Interactive states: default, hover, active, focus, disabled, loading, empty and error must remain visible and understandable.

## Content rules

- Say what the user can do today.
- Keep the Free plan claims factual.
- Describe future announcements, sales and deeper photo workflows as evolution, never as available features.
- Prefer operational language: cadastrar, localizar, revisar, avançar, publicar when the feature exists.
